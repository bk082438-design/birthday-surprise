a letter from me to you

Hey Chota Hathi,

Lots of love to you on your birthday. ❤️

I know that this year, on your birthday, we aren't together. We're miles away from each other, and honestly, it does feel a little bad. This time, I didn't even really have anything with me that I could give you as a proper birthday gift.

So I thought, at least I can do whatever I can to make your birthday a little more special.

I really wish we were together right now. I wish we could cut your birthday cake together, be happy, laugh over stupid things, and do all those crazy things we usually do. But I guess life had something else planned for us this time.

But no, we're not getting emotional here. 😅 It's your birthday.

And for me, this day is very, very special. Not just because it's your birthday, but because it's the birthday of someone who has been such an important part of my life.

You've always supported me. You've given me emotional support, stayed beside me through my ups and downs, and somehow managed to stay even when things weren't easy.

We've had our fights. We've had misunderstandings. We've had moments where things could have easily fallen apart.

But you still didn't leave.

Instead, you stayed, we fixed things, and somehow, you were still there beside me.

And honestly, I'm so lucky that I got you.

That's why this isn't just another birth date for me. It's an emotion.

Because this is the day someone very special to me was born.

And one of the things I love the most about you is that you've never really judged me. Even after everything we've been through, you've always accepted me for who I am.

And maybe that's why I feel so bad when I can't do much for you, especially on a day that's supposed to be yours. I really wish I could do more. I wish I could be there, make the day special for you, and give you something that actually feels worthy of you.

But then you just go,

“aree chal na” 😅

And somehow, you make me feel better about it.

This day is really special. Like, very special.

And I'm genuinely so happy that someone like you was born.

I hope you achieve everything you want in life. I hope you get all the happiness this world has to offer, and I hope life gives you countless reasons to smile.

And honestly, more than anything, I just want to see you happy.

For as long as I'm here.
Till my last breath.

Once again, happy birthday, my mota, my chota hathi. ❤️

Love you always.
